    Data gaps are not zero trees

    Method. The official street network (Straßennetz, Stadt Leipzig) is cut into equal pieces of at most 100 m. Around each piece a 30 m corridor (15 m to each side, flat ends) is drawn. A tree counts for a piece when its stem stands inside the corridor. Each tree's crown is a disc with the recorded crown diameter (kr_durchm); canopy cover = area of the union of all crown discs inside the corridor ÷ corridor area. Classes: very low < 5 %, low 5–15 %, moderate 15–30 %, good ≥ 30 %.

    Missing values are not dropped. Trees without a usable crown diameter, height or planting year stay in the counts. A missing crown is estimated from trees of the same genus and height class (or genus, or city median); the map reports both the estimate and the measured-only lower bound, and marks a piece as a data gap when ≥ 25 % of its trees lack a crown diameter.

    Heat. Land-surface temperature from Landsat 8/9 (USGS Collection 2, band ST_B10), clear-sky summer scenes (June–August), cloud-masked, combined as the median of per-scene anomalies; sampled every 10 m along each street. It is the temperature of surfaces at about 10:30 local time, not air temperature or felt heat. Heat thirds are relative to all Leipzig streets.

    Limits. The Baumkataster lists trees managed by the city; trees on private ground, in courtyards or front gardens are missing, and the city notes that recording is not complete. "No registered trees" therefore does not always mean "no trees". Crowns are circles; real crowns overlap buildings and change with pruning and season.
